# Equipments
## Board types (aka Snowboard Profiles) [from ig Snowboardprocamp]()

Basics: 
    - Camber: Push up from gravity
    - Rocker: Push down from gravity

1. Tradition Camber
    - The original
    - Sections: Nose, Camber Zone (for pop and control), Tail
    - Best: holding edges, harder for beginners
2. Rocker 
    - Best for beginners, easy to turn, catch free (no catch edge that is)
    - Sections: Nose, Rocker (only this part touches snow), Tail
3. Directional Camber
    - Best for powder due to larger rocker nose which is best for powder
    - Sections: Nose, longer Rocker Zone (more float), Camber (pop and control), shorter Rocker Zone (quick turns), Tail
    - Variations where tail is NOT rocker
        - Jones lonewolf
        - arbor padre
        - jones freecarver
 
4. Camrock
    - Best for park, camber part good for ollie, catch free from rocker 
    - Sections: Nose, Rocker Zone (easier turns), Camber (pop and control), Rocker Zone (easier turns), Tail

5. Rocker Camber hybrid
    - Good for quick turns in powder, good for switch in powder. Good for ground tricks
    - Sections: Nose, short Rocker (lifted tip), Camber (pop and control), Rocker Zone (easy turns), Camber (pop and control), Rocker Zone (Lifte tip), Tail

6. Flat
    - Not super well with anything but very prediable. Stable at high speeds 
    - Sections: Nose, Flat (Stable and playful), Tail
